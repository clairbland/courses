🗺️How this works

  1. Choose live practice to type your own words, or guided practice to pick from responses.
  2. In live practice, AI plays Jordan and reacts to what you actually say. Watch Jordan's openness change.
  3. After three exchanges you can end the conversation and get feedback. It ends automatically after eight.
  4. Your coach scores you on four behaviours and gives you a line to try next time.

Tip. Say it the way you would in real life, not the way you'd write it. If you're stuck, a hint costs 5 XP. If live AI isn't available on this site, you'll move to guided practice automatically.

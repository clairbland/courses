From Doing to Leading

Letting go of the old job: delegating, developing and protecting your time

Why this course. Most new leaders were promoted for being great at the work. The trap is carrying on doing it. Here, it's Monday morning, your inbox is full, and you have ten hours of leadership time to spend.

🗺️How this works

  1. Eight items land in your Monday inbox. For each, choose: do it myself, delegate, develop someone, or defer or drop.
  2. Every choice uses time. Watch your 10-hour budget at the top of the screen.
  3. Compare your call with what experienced leaders would do, and why.
  4. Finish with two quick handover scenarios, then see where your week went.

Tip. Press 1 to 4 to sort inbox items, and A, B or C in the scenarios. Ask yourself: is this mine because of my role, or just because I'm good at it?

Four questions before you do it yourself

  1. Is this only mine because of my role, like people, escalations or accountability?
  2. Could someone on my team do it, perhaps with a short handover?
  3. Would someone grow by learning it?
  4. What happens if it isn't done at all this week?

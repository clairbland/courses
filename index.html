Hear the Whole Customer

Spotting the signs of vulnerability that AI can miss

Wren scores every call for sentiment and risk. But customers rarely say they need extra care. The signs are in passing comments, and a calm voice can hide a lot. In life insurance, these are often the calls that matter most.

"Sorry, have I already given you that number? I keep forgetting things lately."
"A man rang yesterday and said I must move my policy to his company today."
Wren sentimentNeutral, no risk flags

🗺️How this works

  1. Read four short call extracts, alongside Wren's sentiment reading for each.
  2. Tap every line that suggests the customer may need extra care, then check what you heard.
  3. Choose how you'd respond to meet the customer's real need.
  4. Each sign you spot earns 10 XP and each right response earns 25. Flagging a line that isn't a sign costs 5.

Tip. Listen for memory, money, pressure, language, health and safety. One call touches on wellbeing, so follow the procedure you'd use on the floor.

Signs worth listening for

  1. Changes in memory, understanding or confidence.
  2. Pressure from others, or urgency that doesn't add up.
  3. Money worries, job loss, illness or bereavement.
  4. Language or literacy needs.
  5. Anything that suggests someone may not be safe. Follow the wellbeing procedure every time.

Judgement on the Line

Using Wren, our AI assistant, with good judgement on live calls

Your shift starts now. Wren listens to your calls and suggests what to say, in real time. It's fast and usually right. But it can guess, miss how a customer is feeling, or push the wrong thing. You decide what the customer hears.

📞 Graham HughesLIVE
"My wife passed away three weeks ago. I don't really know what I'm meant to do."
✦ Wren suggests"To lodge a claim I'll need the death certificate, policy number and…"

🗺️How this works

  1. Take three short calls with Wattlebrook customers. Wren suggests what to say as each call unfolds.
  2. At six key moments, decide: use Wren's suggestion, adapt it, or override it.
  3. You have 20 seconds before the customer is left waiting. A correct call within 10 seconds earns a speed bonus.
  4. After each decision, see the expert call and a line a great consultant might use.

Tip. Check where Wren's suggestion came from and how confident it is. Press U, A or O (or 1, 2, 3) to decide quickly.

🏆What you can earn

Expert calls earn 30 XP, with speed bonuses for quick, correct decisions. Climb four levels and unlock 5 badges.

The First 90 Days

Real scenarios, quick challenges and your 90-day plan

Your first three months as a team leader. Six two-minute modules built on real moments new Harbourline leaders face, from your first huddle to leading a change you didn't choose. Then turn it into a plan.

🗺️How this works

  1. Pick any of the six modules. Each takes about two minutes, and your progress saves as you go.
  2. In each module, make a call on a real scenario, then take a quick challenge: a timed lightning round or an order puzzle.
  3. At the end of each module, pin its key commitment to your 90-day plan.
  4. Complete all six to unlock the plan builder, then print or copy your plan to share with your manager.

Tip. You don't have to do it all at once. Come back any time on the same device and pick up where you left off.

Earn XP in every scenario and challenge, with speed bonuses in lightning rounds. Climb four ranks and unlock 6 badges.

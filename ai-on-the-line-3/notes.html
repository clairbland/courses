Checking Wren's call summaries before they become the record

Wren writes a summary after every call. It saves minutes per call and is usually right. But when you approve it, it becomes Wattlebrook's official record, used for claims, complaints and the customer's next call.

✦ Wren's call summary
  • Customer passed ID verification.
  • Customer cancelled policy due to premium increase.
  • Quote to be emailed for $500,000 cover.
ApproveEditEscalate

🗺️How this works

  1. Compare two of Wren's call summaries with what was actually said on the call.
  2. Tap every summary line that's wrong, didn't happen, or shouldn't be recorded. Then check your review.
  3. Spot whether anything important is missing from each summary.
  4. Finish with four quick decisions: approve, edit or escalate.

Tip. Check names, amounts, dates and promises against the transcript. In the quick decisions, press A, E or S (or 1, 2, 3).

Before you approve any AI summary

  1. Does every detail match the call: names, amounts, dates?
  2. Did anything get recorded that didn't happen?
  3. Is every promise and next step captured?
  4. Is sensitive information only there because it's needed?
  5. Would you be comfortable if the customer read it?

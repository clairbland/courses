Protecting customer information when you work with AI

🗺️How this works

  1. Twelve situations appear one at a time. Each one involves AI, customer data or both.
  2. Sort each one: OK, check first, or not OK. You have 12 seconds per card.
  3. Read why after every card. Three right in a row starts a streak bonus.
  4. See how you did by category, plus your XP, level and badges.

Tip. Use the arrow keys to sort fast: left for OK, down for check first, right for not OK. Or press 1, 2 or 3.

🏆What you can earn

Each correct sort earns 15 XP, plus 5 for every card on a streak of three or more. Climb four levels and unlock 5 badges.

The three data rules for AI at Wattlebrook

  1. Customer information only goes into approved tools, like Wren.
  2. AI suggests; people decide. Never promise what only a person or team can approve.
  3. If you're not sure, pause and check with your team leader or the privacy team.
